Hotel Management System

Reservation Management: Handles online and offline bookings, prevents double-booking, and updates room availability in real time. Front Desk Operations: Simplifies check-in/check-out, guest profiles, and billing. Housekeeping Management: Tracks room cleaning status, assigns tasks, and ensures rooms are ready for guests. Point of Sale (POS): Manages restaurant, bar, spa, or gift shop transactions within the hotel. Accounting & Reporting: Generates invoices, tracks revenue, and provides analytics for decision-making. Channel Management: Connects the hotel to online travel agencies (OTAs) like Booking.com or Expedia, syncing availability and rates. Customer Relationship Management (CRM): Stores guest preferences, loyalty programs, and feedback to enhance guest experience.
